Everything we produced during **Eco Rangers — Journeys of Impact** is now published on the
project page, free to download, share and reuse. Ten publications in total, in English,
Icelandic and Slovak.

## What's in there

**The Sustainable Travel Guide** is the centrepiece — 56 pages on travelling with a lighter
footprint, covering transport, packing, food, accommodation and the everyday decisions that
quietly add up. It comes with a short **cheat sheet**: a pocket checklist for before you go,
while you are away, and once you are home.

Alongside it are the two methodologies, both written so that another organisation can pick
them up and run them:

- The **Training Methodology** documents the training course itself — sessions like Emotional
  Awareness While Traveling, Pack Smart, The Ripple Effect and The Council of All Beings, with
  timings, materials and facilitation notes.
- The **Workshop Methodology** covers the eleven community workshops, from repair cafés and
  clothing swaps to ocean therapy, plogging and mapping local green spaces.

Finally, there is a set of ten **comic posters**, ready to print at A4 or A3 for a classroom,
an office or a community space.

## Why we are giving it all away

A project like this is only worth the effort if what it produces outlives it. The guides and
methodologies were written to be reused — not filed away as a reporting obligation. If you run
a youth group, a school, a library or a community space and any of this is useful to you, take
it and adapt it.

If you do use something, we would genuinely like to hear about it — drop us a line at
[circa.iceland@gmail.com](mailto:circa.iceland@gmail.com).
