Where you keep your money is a climate decision — even if it rarely feels like one. Circa set
out to make that link visible.

Before deciding where to bank ourselves, we examined the environmental credentials of Iceland's
three main banks — **Arion Bank**, **Landsbankinn** and **Íslandsbanki** — and shared what we
found openly, turning a private, everyday choice into a public conversation about transparency
and climate responsibility.

This early initiative reflects a principle that runs through all of Circa's work: sustainability
claims should be questioned, compared, and made useful to ordinary people.

## What we did

- Compared the environmental credentials of Iceland's three main banks
- Reframed an everyday financial choice as a matter of climate responsibility
- Shared the findings openly to spark public discussion
- Put into practice our commitment to scrutinising green claims
