We changed the default search engine on Circa's computers to [Ecosia](https://www.ecosia.org/).
It is a small change, and exactly the kind of everyday decision our
[Sustainable Travel Guide](/projects/eco-rangers.html#outputs) spends 56 pages on. The settings
nobody ever opens end up shaping a lot of small actions.

## What convinced us

The ownership structure, more than the tree counter.

In 2018 Ecosia's founders handed shares to the Purpose Foundation and gave up two specific
rights: the right to sell the company, and the right to take profits out of it. That commitment
is built into how the company is owned, so it survives a change of mood in the boardroom. Most
corporate sustainability pledges have no such backstop.

Some other things we checked:

- Ecosia was founded in Berlin in 2009 and has been a certified B Corporation since 2014, the
  first German company to be certified.
- Around 80% of profits go into tree planting, and increasingly into climate technology,
  renewable energy and biodiversity projects. The counter on its home page passed 258 million
  trees in November 2026.
- It powers its servers with renewable energy and built its first solar plant in 2017.
- It publishes monthly financial reports: what came in, what went out, what was planted.

The reports are the part we care about most. When we
[looked at Iceland's banks](/blog/how-green-are-icelands-banks/) the test was the same. Does the
organisation show you its workings, or only its slogans?

## What it is not

Ecosia is not a privacy tool, and the green branding should not be allowed to suggest otherwise.

It does not build personal profiles from your search history and does not use external tracking
tools. But to return results and ads, your query and IP address go to Microsoft Bing or Google.
If strict privacy is your main concern, you want something else.

A single search also does not plant a tree. Searches earn ad revenue, and a share of the
resulting profit pays for planting. That version is slower and less catchy than the slogan,
which is roughly why we trust it more.

## If you want to do the same

It takes about two minutes. Every major browser lets you change the default search engine in
its settings, and Ecosia offers extensions and a mobile browser if you would rather not dig
through menus.

A default is a cheap thing to change. It costs nothing and keeps working long after you have
forgotten you changed it.
