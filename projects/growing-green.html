About the project

Growing Green Together is an educational collaboration that brings sustainability learning out of the abstract and into daily life — the clothes we wear, the food we grow, the way we look after ourselves and each other.

Across Reykjavík and the Westfjords, Circa hosted and co-created sessions that mixed practical skills with reflection and play. Each activity was designed to become a reusable tool: something other educators can pick up and run in their own communities, long after the project ends.

Highlights

  • Clothing repair and upcycling sessions
  • Planting and growing activities
  • Mindfulness and well-being practices
  • Participatory learning games turned into adult-education tools
