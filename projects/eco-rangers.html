About the project

Eco Rangers — Journeys of Impact is Circa's flagship collaboration with Slovak Eco Quality (SEQ), a 19-month programme exploring how we travel, how we care for our own well-being, and how both are tied to the health of the planet.

Working with adult educators and community members, the project co-creates practical, reusable tools that help people move through the world with a lighter footprint and a deeper connection to place. Learning happens in both countries — from training weeks in Slovakia to hands-on activities at Iceland's Sólheimar Eco Village — and lands back home through local workshops and community events.

Highlights

  • Training programmes in Slovakia and at Sólheimar Eco Village, Iceland
  • Twenty local workshops and collaborative sustainable-city mapping
  • A Sustainable Travel Guide, training methods and comic posters as open outputs
  • Circa's Reykjavík clothes swap & repair café, bringing circular living to the community

Project outputs

Everything Eco Rangers produced is free to download, share and reuse — in English, Icelandic and Slovak. All files are PDFs, and each link shows its size so you know what you are opening.

  • Sustainable Travel Guide

    56 pages · 3 languages

    The project's main publication — a practical look at travelling with a lighter footprint: transport, packing, food, accommodation and the everyday choices that add up, written for travellers and educators alike.

    English PDF · 3.1 MB Íslenska PDF · 3.2 MB Slovenčina PDF · 3.0 MB

  • Sustainable Travel Cheat Sheet

    10 pages · English

    A pocket checklist distilled from the guide — what to think about before you go, while you are away, and once you are home again.

    English PDF · 2.7 MB

  • Training Methodology

    65 pages · 3 languages

    Eco Rangers: Facilitating Impactful Journeys — the full training-course methodology, with ready-to-run sessions such as Emotional Awareness While Traveling, Pack Smart, The Ripple Effect and The Council of All Beings, including timings, materials and facilitation notes.

    English PDF · 4.4 MB Íslenska PDF · 4.4 MB Slovenčina PDF · 4.5 MB

  • Workshop Methodology

    65 pages · English

    Step-by-step methodology for the eleven community workshops — from repair cafés and clothing swaps to ocean therapy, plogging and mapping local green spaces — so any organisation can run them.

    English PDF · 30.5 MB

  • Comic Posters

    4 posters · print-ready

    A set of four illustrated posters bringing the project's messages to life — ready to print at A4 or A3 for classrooms, offices and community spaces.

    A4 PDF · 3.4 MB A3 PDF · 5.2 MB
